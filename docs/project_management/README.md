# AppManager Project Management

## 1. Reading and Authoring Rules

* **Targeted Context:** Before starting work, authors must read the current state of the specific documents relevant to the immediate task.
* **Verification over Exhaustion:** While reading the entire normative hierarchy or clarification register from top to bottom is not required to begin work, authors remain responsible for verifying that their additions do not duplicate existing rules, ensuring every proposition maintains exactly one canonical statement.


## 2. Defect Resolution and Anti-Circularity Rules

* **Direct Resolution:** All defects—including contradictions, semantic-loss defects, and authoritative conflicts—must be corrected directly in the affected document under the existing hierarchy. Do not spawn new correction programmes for future defects.
* **Physical Removal of Duplicates:** Merely tracking or classifying a duplicate is not considered completion. Reduction work must physically remove redundant normative restatements or reduce them to valid references, local bindings, or local deltas.
* **Temporary Vehicles:** Temporary clarification documents are preserved only as transitional tools; their semantics must be integrated directly into canonical primary owners, after which the temporary vehicle must be physically retired to satisfy the single-source-of-truth requirement.
* **Settled Architecture:** Integrating or reducing wording does not reopen settled architecture.


## 3. Continuing Authority and Domain Boundaries

* **Normative Hierarchy:** The established normative hierarchy is unchanged. Project-management records do not become product authority, and similar naming or data shape does not establish semantic equivalence.
* **Domain Ownership and Subordination:**
* Shared capabilities always act as subordinate specialists.
* **Maintenance:** Remains strictly bounded and must not be used as a catch-all namespace.
* **AI:** Ownership follows project-side AI-environment intent. Utilizing an AI Capability does not transfer another domain's primary intent or authority to the AI domain.
* **Coordinated Operations:** Operations remain strictly under the authority of their respective owning domains over DD-1-managed scope:
* *Git.commit* acts on Git-owned application intent.
* *Docs generation/update* acts on Docs-owned documentation intent.
* *Maintenance* acts on Maintenance-owned intent, which is further narrowed by operation-specific eligibility and stronger-owner gates.
